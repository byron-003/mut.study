📌 HOW TO ADD THE MUT LOGO
================================

STEP 1: Save your logo image
-----------------------------
1. Save the MUT logo you uploaded as "mut-logo.png" (or "mut-logo.jpg")
2. Place it in this folder: client/public/
   
   Full path: C:\Users\oyooo\mut-study-hub\client\public\mut-logo.png

STEP 2: That's it!
------------------
The logo is already configured to appear in:
✅ Navigation header (top of every page)
✅ Login page
✅ Registration page

The code automatically looks for: /mut-logo.png

If you use a different name or format, update these files:
- client/src/components/Navbar.jsx (line ~15)
- client/src/pages/LoginPage.jsx (line ~21)
- client/src/pages/RegisterPage.jsx (line ~44)

Replace "/mut-logo.png" with your filename (e.g., "/mut-logo.jpg")

SUPPORTED FORMATS:
- PNG (recommended - supports transparency)
- JPG/JPEG
- WebP
- GIF

NOTE: The logo will maintain its original quality and format!
